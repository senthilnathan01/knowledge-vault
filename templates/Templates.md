# Templates

Copy these templates when starting structured research:

- [[Research Brief]] — question, scope, evidence criteria, and deliverable
- [[Paper Note]] — bibliographic metadata, reading notes, and evidence
- [[Synthesis Note]] — comparison and conclusions across sources
- [[Publication Draft]] — private staging note for a public article

Templates default to private and unfinished. Replace all bracketed placeholders before changing a note to `ready-to-publish`.
