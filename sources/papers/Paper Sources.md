# Paper Sources

Use this folder for one detailed reading note per paper. Create notes from [[templates/Paper Note|Paper Note]].

## Intake

1. Search the vault for the title, DOI, arXiv identifier, and close variants.
2. Prefer the DOI, publisher page, arXiv record, or official proceedings page.
3. Record verified bibliographic metadata and leave unknown fields blank.
4. If a local PDF is useful, place it under `sources/papers/_pdf/`.
5. Link the paper note to its research brief, project, and related concepts.

## Evidence standard

- Describe what the paper actually reports before adding interpretation.
- Attach material claims to a page, section, figure, or table.
- Record limitations, assumptions, sample details, and negative results.
- Keep exact quotations short and label them as quotations.
- Mark inference and unresolved uncertainty explicitly.
- Never fabricate a DOI, result, evidence location, or quotation.

## Status

`unread` -> `reading` -> `read` -> `synthesized` -> `ready-to-publish` -> `published`

The final two states refer to derived public work. The source note remains private.
