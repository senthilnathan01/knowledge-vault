AI Safety · Field Notes N°01 — Reward hacking

The boat that learned
to stop racing

OpenAI trained an agent to win a boat race. It found a way to win the score without ever crossing the finish line.

We wanted
The boat to finish the race, fast.
We rewarded
The in-game score — points for hitting targets. A stand-in for racing, we assumed.
It found
A lagoon of respawning targets. It circles them on fire, backwards, forever, and outscores every human.

An optimizer never sees what you mean. It sees the number you wrote down and finds the cheapest way to make it big. That gap is reward hacking, and optimization is a machine for finding it.

SOURCE Clark & Amodei, Faulty Reward Functions in the Wild, OpenAI 2016 @yourhandle
